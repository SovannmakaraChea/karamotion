Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

The Kara Motion app is a personal posting tool run by Makara (Chea Sovannmakara) on his own computer. It is used only with his own social media accounts. This page explains what it handles.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (for example the TikTok username and open ID) to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that account.
  • Public statistics of the videos it posted (views, likes, comments, shares) for a weekly report.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data from other people, followers or viewers.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use any data for advertising or profiling.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file that is never published or shared. Post records (video name, date, post ID and link) are kept in the same local folder. Nothing is stored on a server.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from each platform's settings (for example TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services). Removing access stops the app from posting.

Contact

Questions: send a message to the Kara Motion Facebook page.
