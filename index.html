What this is

Kara Motion is my personal content project. I design short motion-graphic videos with AI tools (Claude and HyperFrames) and share how they are made.

The Kara Motion app is a small posting tool that runs on my own computer. It uploads each day's finished video, with its caption, to my own accounts on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and Telegram, and reads the view counts back for a weekly report. It posts only to accounts I own. It is not offered to other users.
